Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Skillshop (www.skillshop.in) offers live 1:1 online classes. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

When you book a class we collect your name, your email address, the course and time you choose, and your payment confirmation details. We do not store your card or bank details. Payments are handled by Razorpay.

How we use it

We use this information only to confirm your booking, take payment, email you a confirmation, and create a calendar event with a Google Meet link for your class. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Services we use

We rely on Supabase (data storage), Razorpay (payments), Resend (email) and Google Calendar and Google Meet (class scheduling and video). Each of these processes your information only to provide its service to us.

Google user data

Skillshop uses Google Calendar access only for the site owner's own account, to create calendar events for booked classes. We do not access the Google data of customers or visitors.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep booking records for as long as needed to run the service and meet legal and accounting requirements. To ask for your data to be corrected or deleted, email us at the address below.
